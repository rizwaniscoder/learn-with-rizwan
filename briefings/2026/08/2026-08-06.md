- **EU AI Act Article 101 enforcement live (Aug 2) — GPAI downstream deployers are the ones being asked hard questions this quarter**
  *What happened:* The Commission's supervision + fine powers over GPAI providers (up to €15M or 3% of global turnover) turned on Aug 2. Article 50 chatbot transparency already applies. Signatories of the July 2025 GPAI Code of Practice get good-faith weighting; non-signatories don't. Downstream deployers of GPAI models now need documented risk assessments to prove they built on a compliant foundation.
  *Who can act on it:* Compliance-savvy AI shops, ex-BigLaw/consulting boutiques, RAG specialists in regulated verticals (fintech, health, HR).
  *Why now:* Legal and procurement teams at EU-facing enterprises are drafting the questionnaires this month. First shop into the meeting anchors the SOW.
  *Angle:* "AI Act Readiness Sprint" — model-sourcing matrix, risk-tier mapping, prompt+output logging, deployer disclosure kit. $20–40K per business unit; pipeline to a €5–15K/mo compliance retainer. Anchor on Article 50 (chatbot) and Article 101 (fines). [Beam AI](https://beam.ai/agentic-insights/eu-ai-act-enforcement-august-2-2026-gpai-fines) · [YuSMP](https://yusmpgroup.com/news/eu-ai-act-gpai-enforcement-fines) · [ComplianceHub](https://compliancehub.wiki/eu-ai-act-gpai-enforcement-august-2026-readiness/)
