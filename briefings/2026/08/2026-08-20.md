  *Why now:* Enterprise crossed consumer at $20B+ ARR territory. Bulk of that sits with F500 SIs; mid-market and vertical work is wide open and OpenAI is scaling channel to catch it.
  *Angle:* Sell "GPT-5.6 + Codex + ChatGPT Work rollout — 45 days, 200 seats, one production case study" at $40–80K fixed fee. Feed the case study back into an OpenAI Partner Network Select application within four weeks.

The round came together in <48 hours. Total funding now >$200M.   *Why now:* Every scaling startup and mid-market co with a legacy NetSuite stack is now getting a Rillet pitch. Deployment, chart-of-accounts migration, and audit-trail integration are all outside Rillet's own delivery capacity at 600 customers doubling quarterly.
  *Angle:* Fixed-fee "Rillet migration + 6-month AR/AP eval harness" — GL migration, close-cycle audit, Ragas-style eval on the agentic ledger actions. $30–60K one-off + $8–12K/mo ops retainer. Pitch CFOs directly on LinkedIn — Rillet's ideal-customer profile.
