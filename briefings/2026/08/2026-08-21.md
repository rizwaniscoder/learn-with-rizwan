## 🎯 Opportunity signals
*For founders and service businesses.*

- **Ramp Router.com launches (Aug 20) — LLM cost audit becomes a repeatable service line**
  *What happened:* Ramp opened its internal LLM gateway to the public. One OpenAI-compatible endpoint that picks the cheapest model meeting each request's quality bar. Customers already on it report 40% inference cost savings; Ramp cut its own AI bill 30%. Free through end of 2026, US-only. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/ramp-launches-its-own-ai-model-router-called-router/) · [Ramp Router](https://ramp.com/router) · [Benzinga on OpenRouter positioning](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/08/61344240/ramp-takes-a-shot-at-openrouter-with-new-ai-service)
  *Who can act on it:* AI/data shops with LLMOps chops; any consultancy already running eval harnesses for clients.
  *Why now:* Every mid-market with a >$25K/mo OpenAI or Anthropic bill will ask their CFO "why aren't we on Router?" by end of Q3. The ones without an answer will pay to install and tune it.
  *Angle:* Package a "3-week LLM cost audit + Router install" — inventory calls by task class, define quality gates with an eval, wire Router in, produce a cost/latency/quality report. $12–20K flat; keep the retainer at $3–5K/mo to re-tune quarterly as new models ship.
