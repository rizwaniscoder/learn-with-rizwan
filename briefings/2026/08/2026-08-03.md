Apply to Cursor's partner program while the tier is open. [Cursor announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260730718703) · [Liatrio](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/liatrio-named-as-one-of-cursors-first-official-transformation-partners)

- **MCP 2026-07-28 spec finalized — every pre-spec MCP server needs a migration**
  *What happened:* July 28 spec revision removed the session handshake and `Mcp-Session-Id` header, standardized OAuth 2.0 / OIDC. AWS AgentCore Gateway already ships native support. Client SDKs will start deprecating the old transport within 8–12 weeks.
  *Who can act on it:* Agent/RAG shops with MCP experience, ex-SRE teams who can hold a migration schedule.
  *Why now:* Enterprises that shipped 20–50 MCP servers last year are staring at a coordinated retrofit; first quarter of that spend is this quarter.
  *Angle:* Fixed-fee "MCP Migration Audit" — inventory, retrofit OAuth/OIDC, move to stateless/edge, re-do connectors. $15–25K per server; volume tier for 10+. [Spec changelog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28) · [AWS AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-agentcore-gateway-supports-the-mcp-2026-07-28-spec)

- **Cognizant hits Anthropic Global Premier tier (July 27) — Select tier now the sweet spot for boutiques**
  *What happened:* Cognizant became the third Global Premier partner in Anthropic's Enterprise network with 30,000+ Claude-certified staff. Anthropic's next tier-review window closes October 1; new-cycle admissions January 1.
  *Who can act on it:* Any shop that can hit Select tier — 10 certifications, 2 production customers, 1 published case study.
  *Why now:* Bar to Select is intentionally low because Anthropic still needs geographic and vertical coverage. Once F500 SIs saturate, admissions harden.
  *Angle:* Run 10 staff through the free Claude certification tracks this month; convert one existing RAG or agent project into a written case study by mid-September; apply October 1. [Cognizant/Anthropic](https://www.enterprisedna.co/resources/news/cognizant-anthropic-global-premier-partner-claude-enterprise-july-2026)

  *Angle:* "Gemini on Fusion — first use case in 6 weeks" — pick one high-value module (invoice matching, resume screening), ship a grounded prompt library plus eval harness, $40–80K. Route through Oracle account execs. [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/07/31/oracle-to-make-gemini-models-available-to-thousands-of-enterprise-applications-customers/)

## 🔥 Must-read

- **EU AI Act GPAI Article 101 penalty powers took effect Sunday, August 2.** The Commission can now fine general-purpose AI providers up to €15M or 3% of global turnover for missing transparency, systemic-risk assessment, or code-of-practice obligations. Applies to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI, Mistral, and any GPAI provider serving the EU market. The one-year enforcement grace that started Aug 2, 2025 is over. [EC Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers)

- **OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna 80%, Terra 20% (July 30).** Luna dropped from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per M tokens; Sol pricing held. CNBC read this as an explicit response to Chinese-model pressure and enterprise cost sensitivity three weeks after the July 9 launch. The frontier inference floor just fell through Sonnet 5 and Gemini Flash pricing at the same time. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/open-ai-price-cut-gpt.html)

- **Nvidia + Safe Superintelligence sign $5B strategic partnership (Jul 27).** Nvidia takes equity in Ilya Sutskever's SSI and commits access to next-gen Vera Rubin systems for an "order of magnitude" compute expansion. SSI last raised at $32B (Feb 2025) — this is the first public signal that Nvidia is picking sides among the safety-first frontier labs after the Anthropic and OpenAI capital packages. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/ilya-sutskevers-safe-superintelligence-partners-with-nvidia-to-scale-its-ai-research/)
