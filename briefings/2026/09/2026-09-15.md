## 🎯 Opportunity signals
*For founders and service businesses.*

- **DeepSeek deprecates V4-Pro at 04:00 UTC yesterday; every V4-Pro API call now serves V4.1 Flash at Flash pricing ($0.15/$0.60 per M).**
  *What happened:* DeepSeek shipped V4.1 Flash (552B MoE, 1M context, MIT weights) on Sept 10 and folded the older V4-Pro tier into it four days later — a 4-8× price cut inside existing customer stacks.
  *Who can act on it:* Any services shop with clients running V4-Pro, GPT-5.6 Sol, or Claude Opus 5 for agentic coding or terminal work.
  *Why now:* V4.1 Flash beats V4-Pro on DeepSWE v1.1 (74.2 vs 62.7) and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (90.6 vs 87.9); customers reading the pricing note this week want a migration path before the next invoice.
  *Angle:* Package a 2-week "V4.1 Flash migration + eval harness" fixed-fee offer — pull the V4-Pro `deepseek-v4-pro` calls, run a 200-case regression on the customer's actual traffic, and route the losing 5-10% of cases (HLE-style knowledge questions) to Claude Fable 5.1 or GPT-6 Astra.
  [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash spec](https://llm-stats.com/models/deepseek-v4.1-flash) · [Consolidation note](https://coursiv.io/blog/deepseek-v4-1-flash)

- **Temporal closes $550M Series E at $12.55B on reliability-for-AI thesis with 4,300+ paying customers.**
  *What happened:* Lightspeed-led round yesterday; ARR past $250M growing 200% YoY; OSS installs at 43M (up 134% since January); OpenAI, Snap, NVIDIA, Netflix, JPMorgan Chase named.
  *Who can act on it:* Agent-workflow shops (RAG, multi-step agents, long-horizon research) whose customers keep hitting "the agent crashed at step 7" failures.
  *Why now:* CEO Samar Abbas framed the round explicitly around "AI has raised the cost of skipping reliability" — enterprise buyers are pattern-matching to this narrative through Q4 budget cycles.
  *Angle:* Cold outreach to Series B+ AI startups and Fortune 2000 pilots offering a "port your agent to Temporal + observability" engagement — resumable workflows, replay-based debugging, and a 30-day SLA. Price it at $30-80K per workflow.
  [Temporal announcement](https://temporal.io/news/temporal-raises-550m-at-a-12-55b-valuation) · [GeekWire coverage](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/temporal-raises-550m-hits-12-55b-valuation-as-agentic-ai-wave-fuels-massive-growth/)

- **AIR Security's $50M seed (Sept 1) opens a new "AI agent supply-chain audit" services line.**
  *What happened:* Sequoia + Greenoaks funded a Unit 8200 duo's platform that continuously discovers, vets and revokes MCP servers, skills, plugins and add-ons across an org's agent estate. 20+ customers already, heavy in FinServ and pharma.
  *Who can act on it:* Cybersecurity boutiques and MCP-heavy dev shops in Pakistan/India/GCC that already run enterprise reviews.
  *Why now:* Every enterprise running Anthropic Claude Code, OpenAI Agents API, or a Salesforce Agentforce tenant is one malicious MCP server away from a Wiz-scale incident — and AIR just proved procurement will pay to close that gap.
  *Angle:* Offer a "60-day MCP inventory + vetting" report for mid-market: list every MCP server, third-party skill, plugin and add-on in use; flag the un-signed ones; produce a policy your CISO can adopt. Fee $15-25K, upsell into AIR resell where they can't reach.
  [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ai-agent-firewall-startup-air-security-emerges-from-stealth-with-50-million/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/)

- **Salesforce goes GA on six named Agentforce agents (Casey, Paige, Carter, Marshall, Piper, Fin); Hunter enters pilot with a long-horizon runtime that pursues goals over weeks.**
  *What happened:* Salesforce pivoted from "build your own agent" to seven pre-built named agents this week — six shipping, one (Hunter, outbound sales) pilot-only.
  *Who can act on it:* Salesforce implementation partners, ISVs, and Pakistan-based delivery shops with prior Sales Cloud/Service Cloud experience.
  *Why now:* Named agents ship with default skills; the paid work is the customer-specific skills, the SLA handoffs between agents (Piper → Hunter → Casey), and the CRM data hygiene work that pre-agent implementations skipped.
  *Angle:* Publish a "Hunter-ready in 30 days" offer that scores a mid-market Salesforce tenant's pipeline data quality, cleans it, and stands up 3-5 Hunter skills; charge $40-90K.

  *Angle:* Package a "6-week AI org restructure" for 100-500 person AI teams — role scoping, tech-lead calibration, promotion criteria for a mid-2020s applied-AI org. $80-150K.
  [Fortune coverage](https://fortune.com/2026/09/12/meta-year-of-efficiency-managers-ai-investment/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/meta-applied-ai-division-managers-restructuring-091126)
